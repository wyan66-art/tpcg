Open Problems

For physicists and mathematicians who want to work on TPCG — Three-Phase Cosmology of Gauge Fields. A programme is judged by its problems, not its promises. Pick the problem closest to your expertise and attack it. You do not have to accept the whole programme to work on one problem. The kindest thing you can do to a theory is to falsify it.

One sentence of the programme: all of physics — spacetime, matter, gravity — is the coherence pattern of non-integrable phase factors {W(C)}, selected by thermodynamics. Spacetime passes through three phases: gas (W0, space without time) → liquid (W1, bosons and time are born) → solid (W2, fermions crystallise as crystallions, chirality locks) → mass acquisition (W3, Higgs condensation). Gravity is second-order emergence: strain of the solid-phase pattern.

The verdict bar

Three numbers, one action. TPCG stands or falls on computation, not philosophy. If an explicit action S[W] is written down and three numbers come out right — starting with the fine-structure constant α ≈ 1/137 — belief becomes demonstration. Until then, everything here is labelled exactly as what it is: postulates, hypotheses, and open gaps. No number below is presented as already computed.

A. The core — without these, there is no verdict

Core · Field theory

1. Write down S[W]

Construct an explicit action on the configuration space of non-integrable phase factors {W(C)}: the weight that selects which coherence patterns dominate. This is the programme's missing foundation; without it, the selection principle has no weight.

Progress counts as: a written S[W] with a defined configuration space, symmetries, and a continuum limit. Falsification counts as: a proof that no such action can reproduce the known gauge actions in the liquid limit.

Core · Mathematical physics

2. Define the measure

Make microstate counting over {W(C)} rigorous. The area law for Wilson loops is currently an input, not an output. What is the measure, what are the boundary conditions, and what regularisation survives the continuum limit?

Progress counts as: a measure under which the area law, or its failure, is derived rather than assumed.

Core · Quantum gravity

3. Close the W(C) → metric map

Derive the Einstein–Hilbert action and the Dirac action from the microscopic theory. How does a metric, with its causal structure, emerge from loop variables? How does the solid phase's strain become the metric perturbation hμν, and why is only the transverse-traceless spin-2 mode light at low energy?

Status: [open]. The onset of static gravity at W3 (rather than W2) rests on this still-open bridge and is labelled as hypothesis, not result.

Core · Phenomenology

4. Derive 10−40

Why is gravity so weak? In TPCG, gravity is second-order (solid strain) while the other forces are first-order (liquid modes), so a dilution is expected — but the number αG ~ 10−40 has not been derived from S[W]. Derive it, or show why the dilution argument cannot give it.

Core · Model building

5. Emerge the Standard Model

SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1), the Higgs field itself, and the fermion spectrum must emerge from {W(C)} — they are currently assumed. In particular: why three generations, why the observed charge assignments, and why Higgs condensation (mass acquisition) happens at W3 while the Higgs boson itself is born at W1?

Core · Foundations

6. Quantum weights and the Born rule

Quantisation enters TPCG once, at the substrate, as coherent superposition of {W(C)} configurations summed by S[W]. Make the quantum weights explicit, and derive — or show the obstacle to deriving — the Born rule from that sum. This is the v0.2 programme, after the lattice toy stage.

B. Mathematics — topology, order, and locking

Mathematics

7. Define ξφ rigorously

ξφ, the phase-coherence length, is the programme's unified order parameter: gas → 0, liquid finite, solid → ∞. Give it a definition that works across all three phases, is computable on the lattice, and distinguishes topological order from Landau order. Crystallisation is topological, not Landau — only the handedness choice is Landau-like.

Mathematics

8. Crystallion condensation: mechanism and stability

Crystallions are localised fine structures — quanta of a new matter topological order mothered by spacetime topological order (Wsys = Wst·Wm), not impurities or defects. What condenses them, what stabilises a localised winding against unwinding, and what invariant stabilises a zero-winding localised structure (the dark-matter candidate)? The condensation mechanism is [open].

Mathematics

9. Chirality locking: from fluctuation to a left-handed world

Formalise the Frank (1953) autocatalysis-plus-antagonism skeleton and the Soai (1995) amplification precedent as the mathematics of crystallisation-fluctuation asymmetry: a first fluctuation, amplified, locks chirality at W2. Why left, why only one handedness survives, and what sets the amplification rate? Why is there no first cause, only a first fluctuation?

Mathematics

10. Fractional holonomy: why 1/3, and confinement

Quark fractional charges are fractional holonomies ei2π/3 in TPCG. Derive why the fraction is 1/3 — that step is [open] — and derive confinement as the topological constraint that fractional windings cannot close alone, i.e. the Wilson area law, rather than assuming it.

C. Testable — routes to falsification, no numbers yet

Testable · No numbers claimed

11. High-energy photon dispersion and emergent Lorentz invariance

Emergent c implies approximately Lorentz-invariant propagation, so high-energy photon dispersion is constrained by Fermi-LAT gamma-ray bursts above the Planck scale. Compute the dispersion relation from S[W] (when it exists) and state the bound that would falsify it. A result consistent with general relativity counts as not falsified, not confirmed.

Testable · No numbers claimed

12. Graviton dissolution and the merger spectrum

The graviton in TPCG is a low-energy spin-2 quasiparticle of the solid's strain, not a fundamental particle, and it dissolves at the ξφ–Planck scale like a phonon at the lattice scale. Translate the classical gravitational-wave spectrum (chirp, merger peak, ringdown quasi-normal modes) into the corresponding quanta spectrum and state what a high-energy graviton spectrum would have to look like to falsify dissolution.

Testable · No numbers claimed

13. Dark matter: a0, subhaloes, and rotation curves

Two dark-matter routes are open in TPCG: (a) an emergent-gravity scale effect, testable by deriving MOND's a0 from ξφ; (b) massive, chargeless topological structures from crystallisation fluctuations. Derive a0, a subhalo mass-function cutoff, or a rotation-curve prediction that distinguishes these from each other and from particle dark matter. Open executioners: the Bullet Cluster and the CMB power spectrum.

Testable · No numbers claimed

14. Dark energy: w(z) from delocalised bosons

Dark-energy bosons in TPCG are born at W1 and bypass W2/W3 — no chirality locking, no mass acquisition — remaining massless and delocalised (photon and gluon excluded: photon is known radiation, gluon is confined). Derive the equation of state w(z) ≈ −1 from that picture, or show why it cannot be derived.

Testable · No numbers claimed

15. Black-hole ignition: the merger-rate fingerprint

Black-hole interiors settle as metastable gas seeds; they do not auto-condense — a first-order transition needs a nucleation trigger, and the child's Big Bang is that ignition. Candidate igniters: NS–BH, BH–BH, BH–giant-star, and NS–NS mergers collapsing to a black hole. If parent merger rate is child-universe birth rate, what fingerprint does the igniter leave on the child's primordial spectrum, and how does it divide, in principle, from inflation? Inflation in TPCG sits in the W1 liquid phase, not in the solid phase.

D. Lattice and computation — where to start today

Computation

16. Loop-gas lattice: the sinθ correlator and the critical coupling

The ZN toy first stage is done (four-dimensional Euclidean Z8, Wilson action): β ≤ 0.9 confined-like, β ≈ 1.1–2.5 Coulomb-like, β ≥ 3.0 frozen, and ξφ for the cosθ correlator was ruled out. Next step, awaiting a decision: rerun with the plaquette-angle sinθ correlator over β = 0.9–2.5 with more statistics, and refine the window to decide the order of the transition. The earlier loop-gas runs (Run-09, L = 12, g = 0.05–0.40) found no clean critical coupling; if an edge exists at L = 12 it lies below 0.10.

Computation

17. From toy to non-Abelian

Extend the lattice programme beyond the ZN toy toward non-Abelian loop variables, with a defined ξφ observable at each stage. What observable survives the extension, and what breaks?

TPCG Progress三相进展

A working record of the numerical programme behind the paper — results, verdicts, and what is not yet claimed. The frozen paper itself is unchanged; this page is where new work is reported.这是论文背后数值纲领的工作记录——结果、裁决，以及尚未宣称的东西。冻结的论文本身没有改动；新工作都在这一页汇报。

What is being tested我们在测什么

Whether the phase-coherence length ξφ can serve as an order parameter for the gas → liquid → solid sequence of holonomy configurations {W(C)}. The verdict bar remains: three numbers and one action, S[W]. An explicit S[W], the ~10−40 gravitational dilution, the emergence of SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1), the crystallion condensation mechanism, and the W(C)-to-metric bridge are all still open.检验相干长度 ξφ 能否作为全纯构型 {W(C)} 的气 → 液 → 固序列的序参量。裁决标准不变：三个数，一个作用量 S[W]。显式的 S[W]、约 10−40 的引力稀释、SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) 的涌现、晶子凝聚机制、W(C) 到度规的映射，都仍然是 open 问题。

Loop-gas runs (S[W] v0.1)Loop-gas 轮次（S[W] v0.1）

Later runs use m = 1.2, t0 = 1.0, σ = 1.0, L = 12, cold start per coupling point. The observable is the wrap (percolation) fraction. The lesson of the whole series is that wrap saturates: it stops discriminating once the window is solid.后期轮次统一用 m = 1.2、t0 = 1.0、σ = 1.0、L = 12，每点冷启动。观测量是 wrap（渗流）分数。整个系列的教训是：wrap 会饱和——窗口一旦进入固相，它就不再能分辨。

ξφ verdict: negative for the Re-W connected correlatorξφ 裁决：对 Re-W 联通关联子是否定的

G(d) = ⟨w(C)w(C+d)⟩ − ⟨w⟩² (w = cos loop phase) is at the noise floor (~5×10−4) by d = 2 in every phase, with an effective length of only 0.3–0.4 lattice spacings. There is no finite → ∞ pattern in this observable. This does not disprove a massless photon: the cos θ correlator measures a θ²-like operator, not the photon field itself, so its fast decay in the Coulomb regime means this is the wrong ξφ observable. The identified next observable is the sin θ plaquette-angle correlator — the field-strength correlator itself, exponential when gapped and power-law in the Coulomb phase.G(d) = ⟨w(C)w(C+d)⟩ − ⟨w⟩²（w 为圈相位的余弦）在所有相里到 d = 2 就掉到噪声底（约 5×10−4），有效长度只有 0.3–0.4 个格距。这个观测量里不存在有限 → ∞ 的图样。这并不否定无质量光子：cosθ 关联子量的是 θ² 型算符，不是光子场本身，所以它在 Coulomb 相里衰减快，说明这是错的 ξφ 观测量。已确定的下一个观测量是 sinθ 的 plaquette 角关联子——场强关联子本身，有能隙时指数衰减，Coulomb 相时幂律衰减。

A choice the toy forcesToy 逼出的选择

In this toy, the photon-like regime (β ≈ 1.1–2.5) is not gapped, and the gapped, frozen regime (β ≥ 3.0) has no photon-like slow correlator. The frozen paper's solid cannot be both at once here. The next version must choose: define the solid by the Coulomb regime (the Levin–Wen route) and drop the gapped-solid language, or keep the solid gapped and place the photon in a different regime or stage. Neither option is proven by this scan.在这个 toy 里，光子型区间（β ≈ 1.1–2.5）不 gapped，而 gapped 的冻结区间（β ≥ 3.0）又没有光子型慢关联子。冻结论文里的固相在这里不能同时是两者。下一版必须二选一：按 Levin–Wen 路线用 Coulomb 相定义固相、放弃 gapped 固相的说法，或者固相保持 gapped、光子放另一个区间或阶段。这次扫描没有证明其中任何一个。

Not claimed没有宣称的东西

  • No critical coupling gc and no literature βc for the Z8 Wilson action are quoted.没有引用临界耦合 gc，也没有引用 Z8 Wilson 作用量的文献 βc。
  • No transition order has been determined; no hysteresis run has been done for the toy.没有判定任何转变的阶；toy 还没有做回滞跑。
  • The Fredenhagen–Marcu ratio was not computed: pure gauge theory has no matter endpoints, so it is not computable here. Adding a Higgs field would be a new decision, not a silent extension.Fredenhagen–Marcu 比没有算：纯规范理论没有物质端点，在这里算不了。加 Higgs 场将是一个新决定，不是悄悄的扩展。
  • Literature verification is partial: Creutz–Jacobs–Rebbi (1979) was verified; the Elitzur–Pearson–Shigemitsu paper itself was not retrieved, and is not claimed as verified.文献核实只做到一半：Creutz–Jacobs–Rebbi（1979）核实了；Elitzur–Pearson–Shigemitsu 原文没有取到，不宣称已核实。

Next steps下一步

  1. Add the sin θ plaquette-angle and loop-angle correlators and re-run β = 0.9–2.5 with more measurements, testing exponential versus power-law decay directly.加入 sinθ 的 plaquette 角关联子和圈角关联子，用更多统计重跑 β = 0.9–2.5，直接检验指数衰减还是幂律衰减。
  2. Refine β around (0.9, 1.1) and (2.5, 3.0) with hot and cold starts to determine the transition order.在 (0.9, 1.1) 和 (2.5, 3.0) 附近细化 β，用热启动和冷启动判定转变的阶。
  3. Only after a clean length emerges should any ξφ claim be drafted; only after the order is determined should any βc be quoted. The v0.2 complex weights come after that.只有出现干净的长度之后，才起草任何 ξφ 的宣称；只有判定了阶之后，才引用任何 βc。v0.2 复权重在那之后。

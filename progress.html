TPCG Progress三相进展

A working record of the numerical programme behind the paper — results, verdicts, and what is not yet claimed. The frozen paper itself is unchanged; this page is where new work is reported.这是论文背后数值纲领的工作记录——结果、裁决，以及尚未宣称的东西。冻结的论文本身没有改动；新工作都在这一页汇报。

What is being tested我们在测什么

Whether the phase-coherence length ξφ can serve as an order parameter for the gas → liquid → solid sequence of holonomy configurations {W(C)}. The verdict bar remains: three numbers and one action, S[W]. An explicit S[W], the ~10−40 gravitational dilution, the emergence of SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1), the crystallion condensation mechanism, and the W(C)-to-metric bridge are all still open.检验相干长度 ξφ 能否作为全纯构型 {W(C)} 的气 → 液 → 固序列的序参量。裁决标准不变：三个数，一个作用量 S[W]。显式的 S[W]、约 10−40 的引力稀释、SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) 的涌现、晶子凝聚机制、W(C) 到度规的映射，都仍然是 open 问题。

Loop-gas runs (S[W] v0.1)Loop-gas 轮次（S[W] v0.1）

Later runs use m = 1.2, t0 = 1.0, σ = 1.0, L = 12, cold start per coupling point. The observable is the wrap (percolation) fraction. The lesson of the whole series is that wrap saturates: it stops discriminating once the window is solid.后期轮次统一用 m = 1.2、t0 = 1.0、σ = 1.0、L = 12，每点冷启动。观测量是 wrap（渗流）分数。整个系列的教训是：wrap 会饱和——窗口一旦进入固相，它就不再能分辨。

ξφ verdict: negative for the Re-W connected correlatorξφ 裁决：对 Re-W 联通关联子是否定的

G(d) = ⟨w(C)w(C+d)⟩ − ⟨w⟩² (w = cos loop phase) is at the noise floor (~5×10−4) by d = 2 in every phase, with an effective length of only 0.3–0.4 lattice spacings. There is no finite → ∞ pattern in this observable. This does not disprove a massless photon: the cos θ correlator measures a θ²-like operator, not the photon field itself, so its fast decay in the Coulomb regime means this is the wrong ξφ observable. The identified next observable is the sin θ plaquette-angle correlator — the field-strength correlator itself, exponential when gapped and power-law in the Coulomb phase.G(d) = ⟨w(C)w(C+d)⟩ − ⟨w⟩²（w 为圈相位的余弦）在所有相里到 d = 2 就掉到噪声底（约 5×10−4），有效长度只有 0.3–0.4 个格距。这个观测量里不存在有限 → ∞ 的图样。这并不否定无质量光子：cosθ 关联子量的是 θ² 型算符，不是光子场本身，所以它在 Coulomb 相里衰减快，说明这是错的 ξφ 观测量。已确定的下一个观测量是 sinθ 的 plaquette 角关联子——场强关联子本身，有能隙时指数衰减，Coulomb 相时幂律衰减。

A choice the toy forcesToy 逼出的选择

In this toy, the photon-like regime (β ≈ 1.1–2.5) is not gapped, and the gapped, frozen regime (β ≥ 3.0) has no photon-like slow correlator. The frozen paper's solid cannot be both at once here. The next version must choose: define the solid by the Coulomb regime (the Levin–Wen route) and drop the gapped-solid language, or keep the solid gapped and place the photon in a different regime or stage. Neither option is proven by this scan.在这个 toy 里，光子型区间（β ≈ 1.1–2.5）不 gapped，而 gapped 的冻结区间（β ≥ 3.0）又没有光子型慢关联子。冻结论文里的固相在这里不能同时是两者。下一版必须二选一：按 Levin–Wen 路线用 Coulomb 相定义固相、放弃 gapped 固相的说法，或者固相保持 gapped、光子放另一个区间或阶段。这次扫描没有证明其中任何一个。

ZN toy model, milestone 2 (2026-11-19): order of the first transition (β ≈ 1.01) — undecidedZN 玩具模型，里程碑 2（2026-11-19）：第一个转变（β ≈ 1.01）的级数——未定

  • Method: 4D Euclidean Z8 Wilson action, periodic L4 lattices, single-link Metropolis; hot/cold starts; multicanonical / Wang–Landau sampling in the average plaquette P with reweighting; free-energy barrier ΔF between the confined-like and Coulomb-like states at the equal-weight coupling β*(L). Bin width 0.001 throughout.方法：4D 欧氏 Z8 Wilson 作用量，周期 L4，单链 Metropolis；冷/热启动；在平均 plaquette P 上做多正则/Wang–Landau 抽样并重加权；在两相权重相等的 β*(L) 处取禁闭相与库仑相之间的自由能垒 ΔF。bin 宽统一为 0.001。
  • Canonical evidence for two competing states: cold starts stay Coulomb-like (P ≈ 0.653–0.657) and hot starts stay confined-like (P ≈ 0.62) near β ≈ 1.01; the transition point is bracketed at L=16 in (1.0107, 1.0108) and at L=20 in (1.0103, 1.0116) (up to metastability).两态证据（正则链）：β ≈ 1.01 附近冷启动停在库仑相（P ≈ 0.653–0.657），热启动停在禁闭相（P ≈ 0.62）；转变点在 L=16 被夹在 (1.0107, 1.0108)，L=20 在 (1.0103, 1.0116)（在亚稳性意义上）。
  • Barrier: ΔF = 0.79 (L=8, 2 chains), 1.18(7) (L=10, 2), 1.54(9) (L=12, 4), 3.03(43) (L=14, 2). The L=14 value is the least reliable (25 and 10 tunnelings).垒高：ΔF = 0.79（L=8，2 链）、1.18(7)（L=10，2 链）、1.54(9)（L=12，4 链）、3.03(43)（L=14，2 链）。L=14 最不可靠（隧穿仅 25、10 次）。
  • Fit ΔF ~ Lα: α = 1.89 (formal error 0.19; about 0.35 after rescaling by χ2/dof = 3.3). The forms aL2 + c and aL3 + c fit equally poorly (χ2 = 4.9 and 4.2 for 2 dof).拟合 ΔF ∝ Lα：α = 1.89（形式误差 0.19；按 χ2/dof = 3.3 放大后约 0.35）；aL2+c 与 aL3+c 同样拟合不好（χ2 = 4.9 与 4.2，各 2 自由度）。
  • Verdict: the data cannot distinguish first-order interface scaling (α = 3 in four dimensions) from slower growth. We do not claim a first-order transition.结论：数据无法区分一级界面标度（四维中 α=3）与更慢的增长，不声称一级相变。
  • Also: parallel tempering fails structurally at L=16 (cross-phase swap acceptance ~ 0.06, replicas sort by phase); at L=16 the plaquette histogram can be made flat but the walker tunnels only once in 2×105 sweeps, so a slow variable outside the plaquette limits tunnelling.另：并行回火在 L=16 结构性失败；L=16 的 plaquette 直方图可压平但 20 万扫描只隧穿一次，说明限制隧穿的慢变量在 plaquette 之外。
  • Withdrawn: the old L=8 ΔF of 0.61–0.69 (0.002 bins), the two short L=12 chains (1.76 / 2.16, too few tunnelings), and the L=16 ΔF of about 5 (a single tunnelling).作废：旧 L=8 的 ΔF（0.61–0.69，0.002 bin）、L=12 两条短链（1.76/2.16，隧穿太少）、L=16 的 ΔF ≈ 5（只隧穿一次）。
  • Literature note: the second transition (β ≈ 2.66) is known to be first order (Fernández et al., Phys. Lett. B 312, 305 (1993)).文献：第二个转变（β ≈ 2.66）已知为一级（Fernández 等，Phys. Lett. B 312, 305 (1993)）。

The confined / Coulomb / ordered sequence seen in the Z8 toy is the standard phase structure of ZN gauge theory in four dimensions, so reproducing it does not by itself support the gas / liquid / solid identification; that identification remains an interpretation.玩具中的“禁闭、库仑、冻结”顺序是 4 维 ZN 规范理论的标准相结构，复现它本身并不支持“气、液、固”的对应；该对应仍是一种解释。
